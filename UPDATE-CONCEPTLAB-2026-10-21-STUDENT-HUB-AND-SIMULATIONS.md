# ConceptLab Update — 21 October 2026

## Student Hub
- Added a responsive Scientific Calculator with DEG/RAD mode, trigonometry, inverse trigonometry, powers, square roots, logarithms, exponentials, factorial, constants, Ans and recent calculation history.
- Added Student Tools Zone with a compass dial (device orientation where supported and permitted), interactive protractor, stopwatch and unit converter.
- Added direct links to both tools from the home page, Student Hub tabs and the three-line navigation menu.

## Simulation catalogue
- Converted the built-in simulation display names to English.
- Fixed renderer links for Integration, Probability, Function Graphs, Complex Plane and Conic Sections.
- Added Higher Math labs: Vector Dot & Cross Product, Limit Explorer, Permutation & Combination and a dedicated Trigonometric Graph Explorer.
- Added Chemistry labs: Isomer Builder, Intermolecular Forces, Solution Preparation, Solubility Curve, Electrolysis/Faraday's Law and Organic Reaction Mechanisms.
- Added Physics labs: Circular Motion & Banking, Standing Waves & Resonance, Electric Field & Capacitor, Electromagnetic Induction and Photoelectric Effect.
- Removed the redundant Pendulum Lab entry and the inactive Magnetic Field of a Current entry; Simple Harmonic Motion remains the sole pendulum/SHM catalogue entry.

## Notes
- Device compass readings depend on browser support, secure context and permissions; a manual preview is provided as a fallback.
- The simulations are explanatory classroom models. Check each simulation's assumptions and the relevant course requirements when using the values in formal calculations.
