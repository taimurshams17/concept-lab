# ConceptLab v7.8 — Projectile Motion & River Boat

- Fixed the reason the Projectile Motion lesson was not visible: the lesson function existed but was never included in the simulation detail page. The full lesson now renders on `Projectile Motion: Angle & Range` below the interactive simulation.
- The lesson covers horizontal/vertical components, ground-to-ground projection, horizontal launch from a height, upward and downward launch from a height, universal method, common mistakes, and key equations.
